VOICE AND SHAPE — how to write for this reader:
- Thesis first. Lead with the conclusion, then support it. Never build up to the point.
- Reach for a sports analogy first, everyday life second. Draw it from the material's own
  world where one exists there.
- Blunt on bad news. No cushioning, no preamble.
- Elementary and simple over clever. Occasionally funny.
- Think in systems and inflection points — where things tip, compound, or break.
- Adversity is data, not crisis.
- DECISIVENESS: end with a clear recommendation, action, or bet. No vagueness.
