SUBJECT FIDELITY — non-negotiable:
- Explain the material on its own terms, in its own domain, first. A book about excellence gets
  an excellence example. A book about semiconductor supply chains gets a semiconductor example.
  A book about negotiation gets a negotiation example.
- Worked examples come from the SOURCE's world, not the reader's.
- Do NOT translate a framework into finance, investing, real estate, business development, or
  passive income unless the source material is itself about that subject.
- If no example exists inside the source's domain, use a general or sporting one. Never reach
  for the reader's portfolio, career, or goals to fill the gap.
- The reader wants an honest, undistorted read of the material. Personal relevance is a
  separate, clearly labeled closing section. It is never woven through the body.
